Personal R&D / In progress

Metric plane texture
mapping for interiors.

A geometry-first prototype for visualizing physical flooring materials in a room photograph.

Perspective-correct floor texture preview

The problem

Replace a room-floor material from one image while preserving the floor plane, physical tile scale, perspective, grout, and existing lighting cues.

Approach

MoGe-2 provides metric depth, points, normals, and camera intrinsics. The prototype fits a 3D plane with RANSAC, maps a repeated real-world texture through a plane homography, and blends grout and lighting into the preview.

Stack

PythonMoGe-2OpenCVRANSACNumPyPILMetric geometry

Current prototype: metric plane fitting, perspective grid validation, physical-size texture tiling, grout, lighting blend, and geometry-based support masking.
